# Getting started

Create a workspace for your project, then invite the people who need access. You do not need to link a token to use Telegram, X, Knowledge, or the site.

## Create a workspace

1. Sign in and create a workspace with a name and unique slug.
2. Choose it from your workspace list. The creator becomes its owner.

## Invite teammates

1. In Organization settings, invite teammates by email. Owners and admins can send invitations.
2. Share the invitation link if email delivery is not configured or the email was not sent. Claire creates the link and attempts to email it when delivery is configured.

Invitation links expire after 7 days. Access to workspace data depends on current membership, not simply having a link.

## Understand roles

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Manage the workspace, change other members' roles, and transfer ownership. |
| Admin | Manage integrations and content, invite people, and create developer keys. |
| Member | View the workspace and its connected activity; management actions are restricted. |

Only the owner changes another member's role.
