# Token locks, routers and rounds

## Read token locks, fee routers and rounds

Three read-only endpoints need `token:read` and the organization's approved token source. Each makes no RPC request and returns what Claire persisted from confirmed transactions. They are also MCP tools with the same scope; see [MCP](../mcp/index.md). A fourth, [`token/proof`](claire-proof.md#read-the-claire-proof), does read the chain.

| Endpoint | MCP tool | Returns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/token/locks` | `list_token_locks` | The organization's locks from confirmed funding receipts: `kind` (`time`, `revenue` or `price`), `chainId`, lock `contract` and `lockId`, `token`, `funder`, `beneficiary`, raw `amount`, `cancelable`, `cancelNotice`, `transferable`, `fundingTransactionHash`, `published` and a public `id`. Time locks add `schedule` (`timelock`, `linear` or `catch-up-cliff`), `start`, `cliff` and `end`, and `cancelNotice` (seconds the funder must wait between requesting and executing a cancellation; `0` cancels at once; `null` unless the lock is cancelable). For revenue and price locks those time fields are `null`. `revenue` (revenue locks) holds `router`, `asset` (the quote asset; the zero address is native ETH), `baseline`, `deadline` (Unix seconds) and `tranches` (`revenue` threshold in raw units and `bps`). `price` (price locks) holds `router`, `asset`, `highWaterAtCreation`, `deadline` and `tranches` (`price` target in raw quote units per whole token, and `bps`). `proofUrl` is set once the lock is published. Claimed and remaining amounts are on the proof page. |
| `GET /api/v1/token/fee-routers` | `list_fee_routers` | The organization's confirmed fee routers: `router`, `factory`, `launchpad`, `creator` (the launcher), `payee` (who receives the creator share now; the creator can change it with 7 days' notice), `payeeKind` (`creator`, `vault` or `split`), `payeeTerms` (a vault's beneficiary and schedule, or a split's payees and basis points; `null` when the creator is the payee), `buyback` (pons only; `null` when unknown), `holderBps` (the current holder share; the creator can only raise it), `pendingPayee` (a proposed payee, or `null`), `payeeChangeAt` (ISO time the proposal can take effect, or `null`), `payeeChanges` (the history of changes this app confirmed: `type` of `holder_share_raised`, `payee_change_proposed`, `payee_change_canceled` or `payee_changed`, `at`, `transactionHash`, and `from`, `to`, `payee` or `effectiveAt` as relevant), `projectHolderDistributor`, `claireHolderDistributor`, `treasury`, `launchedToken`, and `totals` per asset (the sums of confirmed harvests, in raw units of that asset: `harvests`, `received`, `revenue`, `creator`, `projectHolders`, `claireHolders`, `treasury`). With buyback on, the launch token appears in `totals` as an asset next to the quote. |
| `GET /api/v1/token/rounds` | `list_token_rounds` | The organization's confirmed project-holder and airdrop rounds, 20 per page (`page`): `purpose`, `distributor`, `roundId`, `asset`, `total` (what this round added), `cumulativeTotal` (everything promised for the asset so far), `previousEvidence`, `recipients` (accounts this round added to), `accounts` (accounts with an entitlement in this root), `snapshotBlock`, `root`, `evidence`, `transactionHash`, `manifestUrl` and `publishedAt`. |

These fields come from receipts Claire confirmed; a change made directly on-chain outside Claire shows in the Claire Proof, which reads the chain. Each fee router also carries `migrated` (true when the router adopted a token launched on pons outside Claire) and `migration`, which is `null` for a router that launched its token itself or has not adopted one. Otherwise it holds `adoptedAt`, `migrationFee` and `migrationFeeToken` (the one-time fee in raw units of its stablecoin), `treasury` and `claireHolders` (the two halves), `historicalPayouts` (per `asset`, the `amount` the router paid the creator uncharged from before the migration) and `transactionHash`.

Lock `id` values are the public proof IDs: `/locks/<chainId>/claire-<lockId>` for time locks, `/locks/<chainId>/revenue-<lockId>` for revenue locks and `/locks/<chainId>/price-<lockId>` for price locks. A revenue lock unlocks as the token's fee router collects revenue, counted from `baseline`, and fully at `deadline`; revenue counts everything the router collects from the launchpad escrow, including third-party credits to it. A price lock unlocks a tranche once the lowest price sampled over 24 consecutive completed hours reaches its target; it is a sampled-price trigger, not an oracle, and everything unlocks at `deadline` (see [Price-target locks](../sources/token-actions/revenue-and-price-locks.md#price-target-locks)). Rounds are cumulative: each publishes a new root for one asset whose leaves carry every account's running total, and a claim pays the account its cumulative amount minus what it already received. A round's `manifestUrl` serves the canonical manifest (version 1) verbatim, and its keccak-256 is the round's on-chain `evidence`; rounds published before manifests existed have no `manifestUrl`. See [Verify on-chain](../verification/rounds-and-manifest.md#the-round-manifest) for the manifest format. Fee-router totals are Claire's record of confirmed harvests, not a chain read; check the router's `totalRevenue` on-chain when it matters.

```sh
curl --header "Authorization: Bearer $CLAIRE_API_KEY" "$CLAIRE_API_URL/token/locks"
curl --header "Authorization: Bearer $CLAIRE_API_KEY" "$CLAIRE_API_URL/token/fee-routers"
curl --get --header "Authorization: Bearer $CLAIRE_API_KEY" --data-urlencode 'page=1' "$CLAIRE_API_URL/token/rounds"
```
