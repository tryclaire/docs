# Notifications

Your notification bell holds workspace updates. Notification delivery is separate from the [Telegram automation rules](index.md) your team explicitly enables; a weekly automation schedule is not a generated report.

## Set notification preferences

Email and Telegram direct-message delivery have per-user preferences by notification kind. In-app records remain visible independently. Failed automation runs, failing X collection, and domain errors queue delivery immediately; other updates queue later.

Telegram DMs only reach accounts that pressed **Start** on Claire's bot. Delivery may be delayed by provider failures or channel limits.
