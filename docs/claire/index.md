# $CLAIRE

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Until a $CLAIRE address is published, Claire shows **$CLAIRE is not live yet** and checks no holdings. $CLAIRE launches directly on pons on Robinhood Chain. Referral payouts, fee-share rounds and launch airdrops depend on the [Claire protocol](../sources/token-actions/fees.md). The rules in this section describe the current implementation and are not a promise of income, rewards or returns.

## What $CLAIRE is

$CLAIRE is launched directly on pons on Robinhood Chain, not through a Claire fee router. Its own trading fees go to the Claire treasury, not to holders. $CLAIRE holders receive **Claire protocol fees only**: half of the 10% Claire takes from the trading revenue of tokens launched through Claire or migrated to it, and half of the launch, migration and lock service fees. The other half goes to the Claire treasury. Amounts depend on activity and can be zero.

## At a glance

| What | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Where it lives | Launched directly on pons on Robinhood Chain, not through a fee router. |
| What holders receive | Claire protocol fees only, paid in rounds pro rata to $CLAIRE balances at a finalized block. |
| Top plan threshold | 25,000 $CLAIRE across your verified wallets. |
| Referral rate | 5% of Claire's treasury share, or 7.5% when your verified holding qualifies. |
| Airdrops | Optional, decided by each project, never promised. |

## The official address

The $CLAIRE token address is published at launch. Until a $CLAIRE address is published, Claire shows **$CLAIRE is not live yet** and checks no holdings, and any address claiming to be $CLAIRE is not Claire's.
