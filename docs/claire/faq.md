# $CLAIRE FAQ

## Holdings

:::details[Why does Claire show "$CLAIRE is not live yet"?]
Until a $CLAIRE address is published, Claire shows **$CLAIRE is not live yet** and checks no holdings. Any address claiming to be $CLAIRE is not Claire's.
:::
:::details[Why is my balance not counted for the top plan?]
Only balances in your verified wallets count, read at a finalized Robinhood Chain block. $CLAIRE in locks, vesting or unclaimed distribution rounds does not count. If the holder data is unavailable, nothing is granted or removed.
:::
:::details[What happens if my balance drops below 25,000?]
Only this benefit ends. Items created above the lower limits are kept and only new ones are refused. A Pro plan the project paid for is never overridden or removed.
:::

## Rewards and claims

:::details[Why is my referral reward not in my wallet yet?]
A reward becomes payable once the payment's block is finalized. Claire's treasury then funds payable rewards in a claim round. Funding a round is not a payout: the reward reaches your payout wallet when a claim is submitted, by you or anyone. A reward also waits while you have no verified payout wallet.
:::
:::details[Why is a referral reward void?]
Self-referrals are void: there is no reward when you belong to the paying project, made the payment yourself, or paid from one of your verified wallets.
:::
:::details[Can fee rounds be zero?]
Yes. Amounts depend on activity and can be zero. Holders receive Claire protocol fees only, never $CLAIRE's own trading fees.
:::
:::details[What if I miss an airdrop's claim date?]
From the **Claims close** date the project's designated wallet can reclaim what is left. Project-holder and $CLAIRE-holder distributions have no expiry.
:::

## Availability

:::details[What is fixed when $CLAIRE and the protocol are deployed?]
These are fixed when $CLAIRE and the protocol are deployed, and nothing above should be read as their answer in advance:

- The $CLAIRE token address.
- The top-plan limits and the 30-day move interval (current values above).
- A paid plan's price and provider, Claire's share of plan revenue and how its referral rewards are settled.
- Who prepares referral payout rounds and which address publishes them in production.
- The production list of addresses excluded from snapshots.
- Whether $CLAIRE in locks, vesting or unclaimed rounds counts toward 25,000.

:::
