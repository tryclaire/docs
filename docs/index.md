# Introduction

Claire is a workspace for project teams to inspect connected community activity and maintain project information and a public site. Connect Telegram chats and an X account, optionally link a supported token, write docs, and keep team notes in Knowledge.

## Start here

1. [Create a workspace](getting-started/index.md) and invite your team.
2. [Connect a source](sources/index.md) to see its collected activity.
3. [Set up your public site](publishing/index.md) when you are ready to publish.

Your personal account is separate from workspaces. Each workspace has its own members, integrations, site, docs, assets, and access controls. You can belong to more than one workspace.
